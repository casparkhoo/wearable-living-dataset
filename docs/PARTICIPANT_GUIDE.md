# Taking part in a study

A short guide for participants. The researcher running your study will tell you how to
install the app and which study to join; this explains what happens.

## Getting started

1. **Install the app** from the link your researcher gives you (normally Google Play
   internal testing or Firebase App Distribution).
2. **Create an account** in the app or the web console with your email and a password.
   Give the researcher the email you used (or the participant code shown under *Your
   account* in the console).
3. **Consent.** Once the researcher has invited you, the study appears in the console under
   *Studies*. Read the consent form your researcher gave you, then press *Record my
   consent*. You cannot record into a study before consenting.
4. **Connect your wearables** in the app (*Devices*). After the first time, they reconnect
   by themselves whenever they are in range, until you disconnect them.

## Recording

- Pick the study on the *Record* tab and tap **Record**. Tap again to stop.
- Tap an activity (for example *walking*) when you start it; labels are stored with the
  phone's own time.
- The app tells you within a few seconds if something is wrong: the ring is not on your
  finger properly, a device stopped sending, or data is being lost. If you wear Even G1
  glasses, the same message appears on the lenses. Fixing it on the spot saves the
  recording.

## Your data

- Your recordings go to the study's server over an encrypted connection, under your own
  account; other participants cannot see or send into your stream.
- Researchers in your study can see and export your recordings for the purposes in the
  consent form.
- **Withdrawing** (*Withdraw* in the console) stops any further recording and removes your
  access to the study. Data already collected is kept, unless you also ask for erasure.
- **Erasure:** ask the researcher to erase your data. They delete your recordings and
  everything derived from them in that study, and the deletion is logged. Copies in the
  server's backups disappear as the backups rotate out (the consent form says how long);
  copies the researchers exported earlier must be deleted by them.
